Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

What are the operating hours of the academy?

We are open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 2:30 PM. We are closed on weekends and public holidays.

Is Milestone Academy a women-owned business?

Yes, we are proud to identify as a women-owned educational institution, which deeply influences our inclusive, nurturing, and empathetic campus culture.

How do I enroll my child in a course?

You can start by clicking the "Enroll Now" button on any course page or by filling out the Admissions Inquiry form. Our team will contact you to guide you through the final steps.

What makes your teaching methodology unique?

Governed by an educator with extensive national and international experience, our engaging curriculum focuses on personalized attention, critical thinking, and creative problem-solving.

Where is the academy located?

We are conveniently located at 65, 15 Jayamaha Bakery Rd, Wattala 11300. You can find us easily on Google Maps using the plus code XVRJ+88.

What courses do you offer?

We offer Advanced Mathematics, Creative English Literature, Foundation Science, and Digital Literacy & Coding. Visit our Courses page for detailed information.

Can I schedule a campus tour?

Absolutely! We encourage prospective students and parents to visit our campus. Simply contact us at 071 777 0808 or fill out the contact form to schedule a tour.

What is the student-to-teacher ratio?

We maintain small class sizes to ensure personalized attention. Our student-to-teacher ratio allows for individualized support and effective learning.
