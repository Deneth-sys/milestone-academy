About Milestone Academy

Empowering minds through excellence in education since our founding

Our Story

Milestone Academy was founded with a vision to provide exceptional education in a nurturing environment. We believe every student has unique potential, and our mission is to unlock that potential through personalized attention, innovative teaching methods, and a supportive community.

Our Mission

To provide world-class education that nurtures critical thinking, creativity, and character development in every student.

Our Vision

To be the leading educational institution in Wattala, recognized for academic excellence and holistic student development.

Our Values

Excellence, inclusivity, innovation, and compassion guide everything we do at Milestone Academy.

Our Leadership

Expert Educational Leadership

Principal & Founder

Milestone Academy is governed by one of the best educators in Sri Lanka, with over 8 years of experience as a school principal in both national and international schools.

Her extensive experience in national and foreign educational systems brings a unique perspective to our curriculum, ensuring that our students receive world-class education while staying grounded in local values and culture.

"Education is not just about filling minds with facts, but about igniting curiosity and empowering students to become lifelong learners and responsible citizens."
